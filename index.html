FAQ

Common questions

Is StoryForge free to use?
Yes! StoryForge is free to download and use. The free plan gives you 3 content generations every 24 hours — enough to try all content types.
What types of content can I create?
StoryForge supports Stories, Articles, Blog Posts, YouTube Scripts, and Emails — with more types being added regularly.
How does the 24-hour limit work?
Your 3 free generations reset 24 hours after your first generation in each cycle — not at midnight. So if you generate at 3pm, your limit resets at 3pm the next day.
Is my content saved?
Yes — every generation is automatically saved to your History so you can revisit, copy, or favorite it anytime. History is linked to your account and syncs securely.
What devices does it work on?
StoryForge currently works on Android devices (version 7.0 and above). An iOS version is planned for the future.
How do I sign in?
You can sign in with your Google account (one tap) or create an account with your email and password.
